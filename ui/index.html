Use the manual connection option

If Google does not allow sign-in in the app window, use your normal browser. The uploader exchanges a one-time sign-in cookie for a local account credential.

  1. Sign in in your browser

    Open Google sign-in and accept Google’s prompts. Leave the tab open.

  2. Copy the one-time cookie

    Press F12. Open Application → Cookies → https://accounts.google.com and copy the value named oauth_token. In Firefox, use the Storage tab. A Google page that keeps loading after sign-in is expected.

  3. Connect your account

    Keep this private, just like a password. It is never saved in browser storage or command history.
